# Letter to the College, the Historical Society and MADRA

The joint version: archive permission, printed copies, contributing to *This
Week in Mallacoota*, and the question of the Mouth's name.

**To:** mallacoota.p12@education.vic.gov.au — the Principal, by name
**Cc:** admin@mallacootabunker.com.au (Mallacoota & District Historical Society)
**Cc:** madra.3892@gmail.com (MADRA)
**Cc:** mallacootamouth@gmail.com — only if that address still works
**Link, do not attach:** https://lovemallacoota.au/archive.html

**Subject:** The Mallacoota Mouth archive: permission, printed copies, and what
comes next

---

Dear [Principal's name],

I am building a public catalogue of *The Mallacoota Mouth* at lovemallacoota.au,
as a community history resource after the paper closed. I would rather you saw
it early than discovered it later.

The Mouth's masthead names Mallacoota P-12 College as producer, with community
volunteers. Issue 1800 recorded that the College holds a complete run from the
first edition on 13 February 1976.

I hold 37 digital issues from 2020 to 2023, all catalogued and searchable by
date, issue number, editor and contents. I have started scanning older paper
copies — the 3 February 1989 *Holiday News*, issue 693 from 2 July 1993, issue
779 from 11 August 1995 — and I have access to about a hundred paper issues in
all.

**Not one page of any issue is published, and none will be without your
agreement.** The catalogue lists what exists; the scans stay offline. The
copyright is not mine, and nothing on the site pretends otherwise.

Four things I would like to ask, and a fifth that is a question rather than a
request. They are separable — a yes to one is not a yes to any other.

**1. May the catalogue stay up?** It reproduces no content: issue number, date,
editor, page count, cover description and contents, so someone tracing a name, a
club or an event can find which issue it appeared in. If you would rather it
came down, tell me and it comes down, without argument.

**2. May the scans eventually be published, free to read?** On any terms you
set — a delay before recent issues appear, particular issues withheld, a
specific credit line, the right to ask for anything to be taken down. Whatever
you set, I will write into the site's published policy so it binds whoever comes
after me.

**3. Would the College or the Society like to print and sell copies from the
archive?** I would be glad if you did. To be exact about what I am offering: I
make no claim on the material and will charge nothing for access to my scans —
but the right to reproduce the Mouth is yours to determine, not mine to grant.
All I would ask is that you tell me how many copies and which issues, so the
archive can keep a record.

**4. Would you contribute to the weekly edition?** Since August the site has
carried *This Week in Mallacoota* — a weekly edition of notices, club and school
news, what's on, weather and tides, local history and profiles of people in the
town. It is the part I care most about, because a catalogue records what the
town was and this records what it is.

There is a section waiting for the things that used to run under "Out and About
at MP-12", and one for the Historical Society's notes. Anyone you nominate can
publish straight to the site without going through me. Nothing is edited, and
nothing is charged for. If a student wanted to write, that is a byline in a real
publication that will still exist when they are older.

**5. And a question I would rather ask than assume: would it be appropriate for
the weekly edition to carry the Mouth's name?**

It is called *This Week in Mallacoota*, and it works perfectly well under that
name — so this costs nothing to refuse and I will not raise it again. But the
Mouth ran for nearly fifty years and the name means something here that a new
one cannot. I am not claiming to be its continuation, and I would not use the
name on the strength of a friendly conversation.

If it is worth considering, there are three versions of it, and I would take
whichever you are comfortable with:

- nothing at all — the edition keeps its own name, which is the default;
- a line acknowledging the tradition, without taking the name: something like
  *"in the tradition of The Mallacoota Mouth, 1976–2023"*;
- the name itself, if the College and the Society consider it theirs to pass on,
  under whatever conditions you attach.

If the answer is no, or not yet, or not without asking others first, that is a
complete answer and I will leave it there.

I am copying the Historical Society and MADRA so we are all on the same letter.
I am one person doing this on my own time, and I would rather build it with you
than around you. Happy to come in and talk it through.

Yours sincerely,

Colin Dixon
Love Mallacoota · lovemallacoota.au
coota@lovemallacoota.au · https://dixon.au

## Notes before sending

- **Name the Principal.** "Dear Everyone" on a permissions letter to a school
  reads as a circular and gets treated as one.
- **Ask five things, expect two answers.** Questions 1 and 2 are the ones that
  matter for the archive. Everything else can wait for a second letter.
- **Question 5 is the one to be careful with.** A name is not a file; it carries
  reputation, and the people who built it may feel differently from the
  institution that produced it. If there is any hesitation, drop it — the
  edition does not need it. Do not use the name on the strength of a verbal yes
  from one person.
- **Do not attach the scans.** Offering the material back to the people who made
  it looks like a fait accompli. Link the catalogue instead.
- **Do not offer reproduction rights you do not hold.** Point 3 says what is
  actually true: no charge for the scans, and the rights question is theirs.
- No reply in three weeks: ring, do not email again. A school is not ignoring
  you; a school is busy.
- Record whatever comes back in `docs/ARCHIVE.md` with the date and who said it.
  A verbal yes from a principal who later moves on is worth nothing unless it is
  written down.

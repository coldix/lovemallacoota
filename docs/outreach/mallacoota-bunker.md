## What they do

Founded 1974. They run the restored 1942 RAAF bunker museum (restoration from 2002, originally an RSL sub-branch project) and a Local Heritage Resource Centre: library, files, research facilities for district history and some WWII military history. Geographic collecting area: Genoa, Gipsy Point, Mallacoota, Wangarabell, Wallagaraugh, Gabo Island.

They sell their own and commissioned books (*Mallacoota Reflections*, *Mallacoota Memories*, *Mined Off Mallacoota*, *When WW2 Came to Mallacoota*). Black Summer 2019–20 PDF is hosted on the site. Film: *Secrets from the Mallacoota Bunker* (Dr Sarah Mirams / Tiny Empire).

Genoa School Museum: Thursday 2–4pm; contact Annette, 03 5158 8253. “Many of the early records are still on hand.”

Collection software listed as a supplier: IST MOSAiC (not a public catalogue).

## Ask them (do not assume)

1. Any Mouth issues (runs, gaps, format)? Catalogued in MOSAiC?
2. If yes: access, copying, and who they believe holds copyright (expect: not them).
3. Current President / Secretary / Curator / Research Officer and which email to use.
4. Research fee, and whether Love Mallacoota may quote/credit them.
5. Directory listing, Tuesday newsletter drop, or member-day presence.
6. Photo/text reuse for displays and the Black Summer PDF.

Collection Policy (2011): no conditional donations or loans; legal title required — relevant if anyone offers Mouth copies.

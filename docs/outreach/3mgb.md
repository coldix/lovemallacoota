## What they do

Volunteer-run community radio. Local music and chat plus Community Radio Network / National Radio News. Listen Live on the website; program PDF also names Community Radio Plus and iHeart. `/listen-again/` exists but the page body is empty.

Public contribution: contact form (including News/Current Affairs and Community Events), volunteer form, SMS, `/event/` for school and non-profit listings, footer newsletter subscribe. Privacy Policy (2015) mentions “Noticeboard, a newsletter” — not described on current pages.

They sell a 2026 Telephone Directory at the Post Office and Foodworks.

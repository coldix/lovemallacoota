# Letter to 3MGB

Tell them about Love Mallacoota / *This Week in Mallacoota*, that we will
print their weekly program, and ask them to tell us when it changes.

**To:** cootafm@gmail.com
**Link, do not attach:** https://lovemallacoota.au/edition.html
**Their guide (checked 31 Aug 2026):** https://www.3mgb.org.au/program/
Radio Waves 2026 V6 PDF, page last modified 26 May 2026.

**Subject:** 3MGB program in This Week in Mallacoota

---

Hello,

I am Colin Dixon. I run Love Mallacoota at lovemallacoota.au — a town
directory and a weekly edition, *This Week in Mallacoota*, after the Mouth
closed. This week's edition is at https://lovemallacoota.au/edition.html

We would like to print 3MGB's weekly program in each edition: 101.7 FM
Mallacoota, 96.9 FM Genoa, and the local shows from your Radio Waves guide.

I have taken that from Radio Waves 2026 V6 on https://www.3mgb.org.au/program/
(the PDF dated May). I am treating it as a Monday-to-Sunday week, the same
week as ours. If that is wrong, or if the grid has moved on, please say so.

Three shows are still marked postponed until July 2026: *The Open Page*,
*Folk Like Us*, and *Good Morning Blues*. If they are back, or if anything
else has changed, a note to this address is enough and I will correct the
listing.

Your own guide says the program can change at short notice. I will keep
using V6 until you tell me otherwise. A line when a show moves, or when
you publish V7, would be appreciated.

If you would rather send community notices the other way — on air from the
weekly — that is welcome too. SMS 0482 170 909 and the Community Events
form on your site look like the right doors.

Colin Dixon
Love Mallacoota · lovemallacoota.au
coota@lovemallacoota.au · https://dixon.au

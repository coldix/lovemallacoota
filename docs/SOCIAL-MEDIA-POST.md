# Social Media Posts — Inviting Mallacoota Locals to View & Contribute

This document contains ready-to-paste social media posts for Facebook, community groups (such as *Mallacoota Now*), local noticeboards, and community newsletters. 

The goal is to invite Mallacoota residents, community groups, local businesses, and historians to **view the weekly edition** and **contribute their own stories, events, notices, and photos**.

---

## 1. Primary Facebook / Community Group Post (*Mallacoota Now* / Local Groups)

> **Welcome to *This Week in Mallacoota* — Read, Share & Contribute! 🌊📰**
>
> Since *The Mallacoota Mouth* stopped publishing, our community needed a reliable, central place to keep up with what’s happening around town. 
>
> **Love Mallacoota** ([lovemallacoota.au](https://lovemallacoota.au/)) is built for the town — free to read, free to contribute, with zero sign-ups or paywalls required.
>
> ### 📖 What you’ll find in each weekly edition:
> - **What’s On This Week**: A curated 1-page snapshot of local events, markets, sports, and community meetups (synced with our public community calendar).
> - **Local Stories & History**: Restored historical photos, bush poetry recitals, and deep dives into Mallacoota's rich maritime and frontier history.
> - **Community Contributions**: Local poetry, photos, audio recitals, and announcements from residents.
> - **Classifieds & Notices**: Local job notices, community wanted ads, and family announcements.
> - **Essentials**: Tide times, 7-day weather forecast, V/Line & bus timetables, and the 3MGB Wilderness Radio schedule.
> - **Print & PDF Edition**: Prefer reading on paper over a cuppa? Download and print the clean 1-page & magazine-style PDF directly from the site!
>
> ---
>
> ### 🤝 We Want Your Stories, Events & Notices!
> Love Mallacoota is created by and for the people who live, work, and holiday here. We’d love to include your contribution in next week’s edition:
>
> 🗓️ **Have an Event or Activity?**  
> Add your event (one-off, weekly, or seasonal) at:  
> 👉 [lovemallacoota.au/submit-event.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/submit-event.html)
>
> ✍️ **Got a Story, Photo, Notice, or Classified?**  
> Submit articles, family news, historical photos, audio recitals, or notices at:  
> 👉 [lovemallacoota.au/submit.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/submit.html)
>
> 📍 **Run a Local Business or Community Group?**  
> Make sure your free listing is up to date in the directory at:  
> 👉 [lovemallacoota.au/directory.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/directory.html)
>
> Have a look around at [lovemallacoota.au](https://lovemallacoota.au/) and let us know what you think. If you’ve got something to share for next Monday’s issue, send it through!

## 3. Targeted Post for Local Clubs, Groups & Businesses

> **Free Community Directory & Event Announcements for Mallacoota Groups 📢**
>
> Are you running a local sports club, community group, volunteer organisation, or local business in Mallacoota?
>
> We want to make sure your upcoming events, meeting times, and services are listed so locals and visitors can easily find you.
>
> **How to get involved (100% Free):**
> 1. **List Your Event**: Submit weekly, monthly, or school-term schedules to our *What's On* section at [lovemallacoota.au/submit-event.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/submit-event.html).
> 2. **Share Club News or Notices**: Got an AGM coming up, a call for volunteers, or a season kickoff? Submit your notice at [lovemallacoota.au/submit.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/submit.html).
> 3. **Check Your Directory Listing**: Verify your group or business details in our local directory at [lovemallacoota.au/directory.html](https://lovemallacoota.au/directory.html).
>
> Help us keep Mallacoota connected! 
> Visit [lovemallacoota.au](https://lovemallacoota.au/) to see this week's edition.

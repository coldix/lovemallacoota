# Government and public services — Mallacoota

Practical directory seed for Love Mallacoota. Compiled 29 August 2026 (Australia/Melbourne).  
**Rule:** every phone, address, hours and URL below has a source. Items that could not be confirmed on an official site are flagged.

This is not a dump of every Victorian department. Include if there is a local presence, a local phone, a local facility, or it is the official channel residents actually use.

**In an emergency always call Triple Zero (000).** Do not use station or brigade numbers first.

Proposed Love Mallacoota IA buckets are noted on each listing: **Services → Government & Public Services**, **Emergency Information**, or **Health & Aged Care**.

### AusNet Services (electricity distributor)

Owns poles, wires and the Mallacoota Area Grid Storage (MAGS) battery that supports backup supply. **Not the retailer** (the company named on the bill). Faults **13 17 99**. Life support **1800 818 832**. AusNet has stated it stationed a registered electrical contractor in Mallacoota for MAGS-related work — operational, not a public shopfront.

**Sources:** [AusNet Report a fault](https://www.ausnetservices.com.au/report-a-fault); [MAGS community update](https://www.ausnetservices.com.au/projects-and-innovation/completed-projects/mallacoota-area-grid-storage-mags-community-update).
